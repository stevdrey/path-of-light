# Playtesting and accessibility

The two-level solo prototype offers independent extra hearts (three/five), frequent authored safe points and falls without heart loss from **OPCIONES → AYUDAS** and **Esc → AYUDAS**. Options apply during play and are saved locally with audio/control preferences. They do not restrict content or rewards. Contact damage can still cause a full level restart. See [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-configurable-assists.md) and [assist evidence](../accessibility-assists-evidence.md); automated recovery checks are not proof of accessibility or balanced placement.

Observe players without coaching for initial comprehension; record the build, mode, assists, active and total time, retries, help requested and observations. Include a range of experience and children aged 5–7 with appropriate adult accompaniment and consent. Define sample size and targets before formal evaluation; a single test cannot establish accessibility for all children.

Telemetry is a proposal, off by default if implemented. Before collecting real data, define consent, retention and an analysis owner; use local review, deletion and export, no automatic transmission, and test with fictitious data. Do not collect names, exact ages, contact details, audio or device identifiers. An exit does not by itself establish frustration.
