# Architecture and state

The prototype includes character components and control strategies, reusable collectibles and hazards, level scenes, menu settings and integration scripts. `GameProgress` is currently in-memory; settings persistence is separate and does not mean campaign saving exists. Level scripts and the current single-character assumptions require review before co-op or durable progression is added.

The recovery implementation separates attempt inventory (score, diamonds and keys) from session records (`recorded_secrets` and `completed_levels`). Ordinary falls keep inventory; depletion and manual restart restore a deep copy of the current level's entry inventory and full configured health (three hearts, or five with the extra-heart assist). Level one starts with empty attempt inventory; level two retains totals from the earlier level. On the final completion screen, the restart action starts again from level one instead of retrying the level. Scene reload restores pickups, crates, player position and checkpoint state. Session records survive all these resets, including starting again from level one, but are not written to disk. Portal entry records the prototype secret visit; an exit records completion after its transition/celebration. Secrets remain optional.

`GameSettings` owns three independent, default-off assist preferences and persists them locally with audio and control settings. Live changes update the heart cap and checkpoint policy; no-fall-damage suppresses damage while retaining relocation. Changing frequency retains the current visited point for the attempt. Settings do not restrict rewards or completion. See [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-configurable-assists.md) for the prototype contract and evaluation boundaries.

See [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-attempt-recovery.md) and the [recovery evidence](../attempt-recovery-evidence.md) for the implemented solo contract. The versioned campaign save model and co-op ownership in ADRs 0001/0002 remain proposals; this in-memory boundary does not establish restart-of-application persistence or co-op behavior.

`GameProgress` owns records for the process session. Returning to the menu and selecting **JUGAR** begins another attempt from level one while keeping records; it is not a destructive new campaign. No current UI exposes a full reset. A future explicit fresh-campaign action must call `GameProgress.reset()`. The menu/attempt distinction is covered by `restart_lifecycle_integration`.

Deferred restart/replay requests check their result. A failed retry rolls inventory back to the still-live scene and releases restart/input locks; retry and pause remain available, including while defeated. A failed final-screen replay retains its completed state and replay input. The level reports an error instead of silently keeping a pending restart forever.
