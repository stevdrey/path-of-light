# Roadmap

Status: proposed, 2026-11-06. No dates, budget or release promise have been agreed. The [GDD](../GDD.md) is authoritative for intended experience; the current build is a prototype, not proof of the target experience.

| Stage | Outcome | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Baseline | Measure the two existing levels, inventory rights, establish test and device plans. | Recorded measurements, asset inventory, reproducible baseline, unresolved risks. |
| 1. Representative slice | Four-level first world, solo controls, assists, map and save, co-op and one discovery-path prototype. | All slice routes and transitions exercised solo and co-op; automated results and human observations recorded. |
| 2. Evaluation and scope gate | Observe varied players, including children aged 5–7 with appropriate adult accompaniment and consent. | Recorded comprehension, retries, active/total time, help and enjoyment; explicit decision on scope and resources. Do not treat 32 levels as approved until this gate. |
| 3. Campaign | Produce approved remaining worlds, endings and progression. | Each approved level has a distinct purpose, optional secret and tested solo/co-op main route. |
| 4. Replay and release | Complete approved rewards, accessibility, localization, licenses and distribution tests. | Save/reload, real device/browser, regression, usability and rights evidence meets agreed release criteria. |

## Dependency order

Baseline and rights inventory start immediately. Recovery and save contracts precede map/rewards; player ownership and state precede cooperative camera, recovery and exits. The slice integrates these streams; observation and a documented go/no-go decision precede mass content production. Telemetry is optional and requires a policy and consent before any real data collection. Real-world discovery facts require sources; fictional and development stories must be identifiable and accurate.

## Scope and risks

The GDD proposes eight worlds of four levels, about two hours, and eight discovery paths; quantities and names remain subject to testing. If scope is infeasible, reconsider level count and extras while preserving a complete, satisfying adventure. Existing generated sound and visual asset rights are unresolved; desktop and browser behavior, co-op, save persistence and child accessibility have not been verified. See [backlog](backlog/README.md) and [wiki](wiki/Home.md).
