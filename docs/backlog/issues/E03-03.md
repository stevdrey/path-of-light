## Implemented persistence boundary

Schema 2 retains discovery, world-reward and character-unlock records, migrates v0/v1 saves, and exposes the existing chamber discovery through the map. The user chose to keep world/campaign rewards locked until explicitly authored finales exist. Synthetic fixtures test reward grants, reload and replay without claiming production campaign content or playable character selection. See [ADR 0007](../../adr/0007-discoveries-rewards.md) and [evidence and remaining validation](../../campaign-rewards-evidence.md).
